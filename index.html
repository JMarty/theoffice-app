TheOffice

Personal back-office assistant

TheOffice is a private, self-hosted assistant used by its owner, Márton Janklovics, to help manage his own email, calendar, documents and tasks. It runs on the owner's own server and is not offered to the public.

It connects to the owner's own Google account through Google's OAuth sign-in, with the owner's explicit consent, and uses the Google APIs only to read and organise the owner's own data and to prepare drafts at his request.
