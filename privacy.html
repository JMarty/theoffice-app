Privacy policy

TheOffice · last updated 24 October 2026

Who runs this app

TheOffice is a private, self-hosted assistant operated by Márton Janklovics for his own personal use. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's explicit consent through Google's OAuth sign-in, TheOffice can access the signed-in user's Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks, Contacts and Forms data. Only the scopes the user approves on the Google consent screen are used.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the account owner: summarising and organising email, reading the calendar, finding documents, preparing draft replies and reminders. Nothing is sent or changed without the owner's request.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used to train generic AI or machine-learning models. To produce answers, relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider the owner uses (Anthropic), only as needed to perform the owner's request.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and any working files are stored on the owner's own server, readable only by the owner's account. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

TheOffice's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
